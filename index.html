Undergraduate Research Assistant — Text-to-SQL & Data Agents

Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University | Apr. 2026 – Present

Supervisor: Dr. Bin Chen

Conducting ongoing research on LLM-based Text-to-SQL and Data Agents, focusing on schema linking, agentic reasoning, and self-reflective SQL generation. As part of this research, I participated in XJTLU's three-month Summer Undergraduate Research Fellowship (SURF) in Summer 2026.

  • Benchmarking & Evaluation: Established baseline results and reproduced representative Text-to-SQL methods, including DIN-SQL, DFIN-SQL, SQL-of-Thought, and AutoLink. Conducted experiments on the Spider and BIRD benchmarks to evaluate their performance.
  • Schema Linking & Reasoning: Explored schema linking strategies, database exploration techniques, and ontology-based representations. Performed systematic error analyses to identify challenges in schema understanding and SQL generation.
  • SURF Research Project (Summer 2026): Enhancing Text-to-SQL with Self-Reflective Retrieval-Augmented Generation. Conducted experiments and contributed to the research methodology and manuscript writing of RASR-SQL, a multi-agent framework combining retrieval-augmented generation with self-reflective SQL correction.

Manuscript: RASR-SQL: Enhancing Text-to-SQL with Multi-Agent Self-Reflective Retrieval-Augmented Generation (Submitted to MLNLP 2026).
